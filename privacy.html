Privacy notice

Data Visualization with R — Base Graphics · last updated 4 October 2026

This notice covers viz-base.rsquaredacademy.com, the online edition of Data Visualization with R: Base Graphics. The book is free to read and requires no account, no login and no payment.

What this site collects

The book itself collects nothing. There is no comment system, no newsletter signup, no user account and no email field anywhere on the site, so nothing you do here identifies you to us.

The one exception is optional, off-by-default website analytics, described below.

Analytics

Nothing is sent to Google unless you click "Accept analytics". A banner appears on your first visit. Until you accept, no request is made to Google, no analytics cookie is set, and no measurement data is transmitted. Declining is equally easy, and you can change your mind afterwards using the “Analytics preferences” button in the bottom-left corner of every page.

The code that runs in your browser

The interactive playground at the end of Chapters 2 to 4 runs R in your browser through WebAssembly. It executes entirely on your machine: your keystrokes and the data you type stay in your browser and are never sent anywhere. No package downloads are needed, because the book's plotting code uses nothing outside base R.

All site fonts, stylesheets and scripts are served from this domain. No content is loaded from a third-party CDN.

The hosting provider

The site is published via GitHub Pages. GitHub serves the files and, like any web server, necessarily sees the IP address and user agent of each request in order to respond. GitHub's handling of that data is covered by the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Analytics-level rights are best exercised directly with Google through your Google Account's data and privacy controls.
